CRITERION CUSTOMER SUPPORT QUALITY POLICY
Version 2.0 | Effective: January 2026
===========================================

SECTION 1: AGENT GREETING AND IDENTIFICATION

1.1 Every agent MUST introduce themselves by full name at the start of every call.
Example: "Thank you for calling [Company]. My name is [Agent Name]. How can I help you today?"

1.2 Agents MUST state the company name within the first 10 seconds of the call.

1.3 For inbound calls, agents MUST verify the customer's identity before accessing any account information.
Acceptable verification methods: date of birth, last 4 digits of account number, registered email address.

1.4 Agents must NEVER skip the greeting or go directly to problem-solving without introduction.

SECTION 2: CUSTOMER VERIFICATION

2.1 Identity verification is MANDATORY before disclosing any account details.

2.2 Agents must attempt verification at least twice before escalating to a supervisor.

2.3 Agents must NEVER share account balances, order details, or personal information without successful verification.

2.4 If a customer refuses verification, the agent must politely explain policy and offer to assist with general queries only.

SECTION 3: TONE AND PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

3.1 Agents MUST maintain a professional, calm, and respectful tone at all times.

3.2 Agents MUST NOT use derogatory, dismissive, or offensive language under any circumstances.

3.3 Agents MUST NOT interrupt customers mid-sentence. Allow customers to finish speaking.

3.4 Agents MUST use empathetic language when a customer expresses frustration:
Acceptable: "I understand your frustration and I will do my best to help."
Unacceptable: "I can't help with that", "That's not my department", "Deal with it."

3.5 Agents MUST NOT make personal remarks or engage in non-business conversations for more than 30 seconds.

SECTION 4: RESOLUTION AND ESCALATION

4.1 Agents MUST offer a concrete resolution or next step on EVERY call.

4.2 Unresolved issues MUST be escalated to a supervisor within 5 minutes.

4.3 Agents MUST NEVER promise outcomes they are not authorized to deliver, including:
- Refunds above $50 without manager approval
- Service credits or discounts without authorization
- Same-day delivery guarantees
- Legal advice or commitments

4.4 If a customer requests to speak to a supervisor, the agent MUST honor that request within 2 minutes.

SECTION 5: CALL CLOSING

5.1 Agents MUST summarize the resolution or actions taken before ending the call.

5.2 Agents MUST ask "Is there anything else I can help you with?" before closing.

5.3 Agents MUST thank the customer for calling and use a professional closing:
Example: "Thank you for choosing [Company]. Have a great day!"

5.4 Agents MUST NOT end the call while the customer is still speaking.

SECTION 6: DATA PRIVACY AND COMPLIANCE

6.1 Agents MUST NOT discuss customer account details in public or with unauthorized persons.

6.2 Call recordings are subject to data protection regulations. Agents MUST inform customers if the call is recorded.

6.3 Agents MUST NEVER request passwords or full card numbers verbally.

6.4 PCI DSS compliance: Credit card information may only be collected via the secure IVR system, never verbally by an agent.

6.5 GDPR compliance: Customers have the right to request data deletion. Any such request must be documented and escalated to the Data Team within 24 hours.

SECTION 7: FIRST CALL RESOLUTION (FCR)

7.1 The target First Call Resolution rate is 75% or above.

7.2 Agents MUST exhaust all available resources (knowledge base, tools, supervisor consultation) before referring a customer to a callback.

7.3 Callbacks MUST be scheduled within 24 hours if FCR cannot be achieved.

SECTION 8: PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

8.1 Average Handle Time (AHT) target: ≤ 8 minutes per call.

8.2 Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) target: ≥ 4.0 / 5.0.

8.3 Quality Assurance (QA) target score: ≥ 7.0 / 10.0.

8.4 Compliance score target: ≥ 8.0 / 10.0. Agents scoring below 6.0 must complete mandatory retraining.
